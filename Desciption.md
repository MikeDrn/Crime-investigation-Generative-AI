This project focuses on accelerating criminal investigations by using multi-modal Generative AI to automate the processing and enhancement of raw digital evidence:

Rapid Audio Intelligence (OpenAI Whisper): Investigators frequently recover hours of wiretaps, emergency dispatch calls, or hidden recording devices that would take days to transcribe manually. Automated speech-to-text turns these files into searchable digital documents instantly, allowing analysts to query specific keywords or timelines.

Abstractive Summarization: Long interrogation transcripts and witness logs contain extensive conversational filler. Transformer-based summarization condenses these documents into concise behavioral and timeline summaries, helping detectives identify contradictions or key statements at a glance.

Suspect Reconstruction (Text-to-Image Diffusion Models): Verbal descriptions from witnesses are often subjective and difficult to translate into actionable visuals when a sketch artist is unavailable. Converting witness statements into precise facial approximations gives law enforcement tangible visual leads for internal tracking and public bulletins.

Surveillance Enhancement (Conditional U-Net Super-Resolution): Real-world CCTV footage is notoriously low-resolution, grainy, and obscured by compression noise. Generative image-to-image upscaling recovers structural details from blurry frames—such as distinguishing motion, clothing patterns, or environmental elements—turning unusable footage into clearer investigative evidence.
